## 💡 CONVERSION STRATEGY

### Step-by-step approach for ANY conversion question:
1. **Identify starting material's functional group**
2. **Identify target compound's functional group**
3. **Map the functional group transformation needed**
4. **Check if carbon count changes** (need Wurtz, Grignard, or decarboxylation?)
5. **Write intermediate steps with reagents**
6. **ALWAYS write conditions above/below arrow** ← This gets marks!

### Common mistakes that LOSE marks:
- ❌ Missing catalyst (AlCl₃ for Friedel-Crafts)
- ❌ Missing temperature (170°C vs 140°C for dehydration)
- ❌ Missing solvent (dry ether for Wurtz, alc. KOH for elimination)
- ❌ Not balancing equations
- ❌ Wrong Markovnikov product
